// THE RULES OF SEIGIESE — the law before the tools. everything built in this dock answers to them.
01The 3-Step Rule. If it takes more than 3 steps to reach or complete the thing a design was built for, it's wrong. Rework it. Redesign it. Figure. It. Out.
02The 5-Minute Rule. If 5 minutes of looking at it or playing with it isn't enough to understand what it is and how it works, it's wrong. Rework it. Redesign it. Figure. It. Out.
03Mental Accessibility. ADHD, Autism, Dyslexia, and other conditions that alter the brain's structure or chemistry are not edge cases. They are users. When a design that passes Rules 1 and 2 still creates friction for them, find the pain points and change what needs to change. Not every condition can be fully accommodated. None will be ignored where they can be.
04Parts of a Whole. Where the design allows, build in parts. No component should be so entangled with the whole that changing one thing means rebuilding everything.
05Diegetic Tutorials. All tutorials must be intuitive and diegetic to their respective creations. The thing teaches itself from inside itself. If learning it requires leaving it, the tutorial is wrong. Rework it. Redesign it. Figure. It. Out.
06Readable Documentation. All documentation must be understandable by middle-school readers and those with moderate to severe Dyslexia. Short sentences. Plain words. One idea at a time. If the docs need a glossary to survive, the docs failed first.
